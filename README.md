# guthrie-snow-globe

A snow globe of the Guthrie Theater in Minneapolis. The light inside follows the real sun over Minneapolis, and the weather inside follows the real weather there. When it snows in Minneapolis, it snows in the globe.

Live page: https://lucybichakhchyan.github.io/guthrie-snow-globe/

## What it does

- **Live:** the globe shows the real time and weather in Minneapolis.
- **The switch:** previews day, sunset and night.
- **Let it snow and Purple rain:** preview the weather.
- **Shake:** press the button, or flick the globe, and the snow swirls and settles.
- **Drag:** turns the globe. The left and right arrow keys turn it too.

## The files

- `index.html`: the page and its styles.
- `switcher.js`: the Light, Sunset and Dark switch, and the sounds.
- `snow-globe.js`: the globe, the light and the weather. The text on the two roof masts is set in `SIGNS` at the top of this file.
